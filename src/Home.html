Welcome to MaiGuide

Welcome to MaiGuide, a guide designed as a point of reference for people to start their journey into playing Maimai.

This page aims to provide a full English guide on how to play Maimai, as most of the resources online are written in Japanese. We want to create a better community, so we hope that this guide starts beginners in the right direction for learning and improvement.

Please feel free to share this website for anyone who is looking to start playing, or for people who are looking to improve.

For experienced players, we also have tips and advice to help players of any skill level to improve. Check out the intermediate or advanced tab!

This guide is written for players playing the official Maimai Deluxe International Version. We will not provide any support for modded or private cabinets.

Please continue by clicking the tabs in the navigation bar on the left of the webpage. Feel free to message the authors for any suggestions.
